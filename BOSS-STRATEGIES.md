# Boss strategies from the forum topic

Source: [BossTeam - How to defeat every boss in game](https://pokemonrevolution.net/forum/topic/224239-bossteam-how-to-defeat-every-boss-in-game/)

I reviewed all three topic pages. The original post supplies the baseline strategy and boss roster; later replies report exceptions, failures, and alternate approaches. Reader suggestions below are identified as such and are not presented as independently verified.

## Default team and sequence

The original post recommends this setup for most bosses:

- **Whimsicott:** Prankster is mandatory; Lagging Tail; Switcheroo, Memento, and Cotton Spore. The screenshot shows Bold, but the author says the nature is not very important. Its screenshot shows 252 HP / 204 Defense / 54 Speed EVs.
- **Shuckle:** Sturdy; no training required for the usual strategy. Use Sweet Scent, Stealth Rock, and Flash; the other moves are optional. Another Sturdy Pokémon with Sweet Scent and a hazard move can substitute.
- **Golduck:** Cloud Nine, Timid, Leftovers; Hypnosis, Substitute, Flash, and Simple Beam. Its screenshot shows 252 HP / 252 Speed / 6 Defense EVs.
- **Ninjask:** Speed Boost, Jolly, Leftovers; Protect, Substitute, Swords Dance, and Baton Pass. The author explicitly corrects the screenshot's Attack investment: train 252 Speed, 252 HP, and 6 Defense.
- **Gorebyss:** Swift Swim, Jolly, Leftovers; Substitute, Shell Smash, Aqua Ring, and Baton Pass. It is the backup sweeper if the first one is knocked out.
- **Pangoro:** Mold Breaker, Adamant, Focus Sash; train Attack and Speed. The screenshot shows Power Trip, Earthquake, Rock Slide, and Bullet Punch, with 252 Attack / 252 Speed / 4 HP EVs.

The Mega Banette screenshot shows Adamant, Frisk, Banettite, and Protect, Disable, Destiny Bond, and Phantom Force.

1. Start with Whimsicott and use Switcheroo; use Memento if it survives.
2. Send Shuckle in next. Use Sweet Scent, then Stealth Rock; use Flash if Shuckle remains alive.
3. Use Golduck's Hypnosis, then Substitute. If the opponent wakes, repeat Hypnosis and Substitute. While it sleeps, use Flash until its accuracy has been lowered six times. Simple Beam is also in Golduck's set.
4. Let Golduck faint, or Hypnosis once more and switch to a sweeper. Set up three Swords Dances (or three Shell Smashes) behind Substitute; Protect can recover HP with Leftovers.
5. Baton Pass to Pangoro while behind Substitute, then use Power Trip. The general instructions call out Rock Slide instead against Thor's Zapdos.

The author says Sweet Scent makes Hypnosis 100% accurate. The author also warns that some Mega-opening bosses can make Switcheroo fail and cause Shuckle to flinch before Sweet Scent; Mega Swampert using Waterfall is the example. For a Mega opener, the prescribed alternative is Mega Banette with Banettite and Destiny Bond on the Mega Pokémon. If the boss switches, attack with Phantom Force, Sucker Punch, or another move and let Banette faint before continuing with the default sequence.

For Maribela and Urahara, the author requires the whole team at level 100. A low-level Shuckle is not sufficient; use a level-100 Shuckle or another level-100 Sweet Scent user holding Focus Sash. The original post also calls out obstacle tools (Pick Axe, Tree Axe, Shovel, Abseil Rope, and Sturdy Hiking Boots).

## Other replies

One commenter offered a different general Baton Pass team: Prankster Whimsicott (Switcheroo, Cotton Spore, Memento; Lum Berry against Mega openers, otherwise Lagging Tail), Golduck (Simple Beam, Flash, Hypnosis, Soak; Focus Sash), Sturdy Shuckle (Sweet Scent, Flash, Stealth Rock, Final Gambit; Lum Berry), Sturdy Skarmory (Flash, Spikes, Counter, Rock Smash; Lum Berry), Venomoth (Substitute, Sleep Powder, Quiver Dance, Baton Pass; Leftovers), and Modest Gardevoir (Moonblast, Hypnosis, Stored Power; Focus Sash). The commenter says Gardevoir should have 25+ Special Attack, preferably; this is their alternative, not the original set.

Another reply describes an Ash Westbrook team using Blissey, Ferrothorn, and Skarmory for the sequence after Dialga: Toxic and healing against Reshiram, Iron Defense three times on Ferrothorn against Arceus, Toxic/Gyro Ball against the next opponent, and Toxic/healing against Kyogre and Lugia. It warns to Protect against Reshiram's Rock move after five Draco Meteors and that rain-boosted Surf can still hurt Blissey. The same commenter lists Mimikyu as a possible fallback against Kyogre/Lugia and Heatran, Garchomp, or a strong Grass Pokémon as optional sixth-slot choices.
